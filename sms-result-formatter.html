How to use Result SMS

1. Choose exam and board

Select SSC or HSC, then choose the correct education board from the list.

2. Enter roll and year

Enter your roll number. The year list automatically starts from the current calendar year and includes years back to 2020.

3. Check the SMS preview

The message updates automatically. Review the exam code, board code, roll number, and year before sending.

4. Copy or open SMS

Copy SMS copies the message. Open SMS app opens your device's messaging app with the selected recipient and message, where supported.

Switch appearance

Use the moon/sun button at the top or Dark mode in the bottom navigation to switch between light and dark themes.

This is an unofficial message-formatting tool. It does not fetch results, guarantee delivery, or confirm that a recipient number is currently active. Always follow official exam instructions.
